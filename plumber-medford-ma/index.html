Medford at a glance

Medford is a Middlesex County city of about 59,700 residents at the 2020 census, with the Mystic River running through its middle. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, and the standard ZIP code is 02155.

Medford's housing mix and what it does to plumbing

Medford has about 25,800 housing units spread across neighborhoods built in very different decades. Medford Square, where Main Street meets High Street, is the central locale. West Medford has a commuter rail stop, South Medford includes the Hillside area and Wellington, in East Medford, has the Orange Line station of the same name.

Age shows up inside walls. Houses from before the mid 1900s are likely to have cast iron drains and galvanized or older copper supply lines, and some have been partly updated. If your place has had two leaks in the same wall, ask a plumber about the rest of that run. A look at the leak detection page explains how hidden leaks are found without opening whole rooms.

Many Medford homes have basements that hold the heater, the washer and a floor drain. That is where most emergencies start, and the water heater repair page covers the usual causes. A tank that leaks from the body should be replaced, not patched.

Lead service lines and Medford's replacement program

Medford's 2022 water quality report reported hundreds of full and partial lead service lines and a required yearly replacement share, with a homeowner rebate offered for a planned round of replacements. Programs change, so confirm current terms with the Medford Water Division before you commit to work.

For homeowners, the practical step is simple. Find out what material your line is, ask the city about its schedule, and coordinate any house side work with the city side so the line is replaced in one project. The water service line page explains the process and the questions to ask.

Before the plumber arrives: burst pipe or active leak in Medford

A short routine limits damage in a Medford basement or kitchen.

Find the main

Locate the main shutoff near the meter. If it will not turn, call the city's 24/7 water and sewer line for the street shutoff too.

Turn off the water heater

Close its cold supply and switch off the breaker or set gas to pilot if the tank is leaking.

Protect what you can

Lift boxes and electronics off the floor, and take photos for insurance before you clean up.
