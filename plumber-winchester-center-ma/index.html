Winchester Center at a glance

The Winchester Center Historic District was added to the National Register on November 21, 1986. It covers about 40 acres, roughly bounded by Mount Vernon and Washington Streets, Waterfield Road, and Church and Main Streets, and the listing describes it as the area's commercial and civic heart.

  • Town Hall (1887), the Brown and Stanton Block (1879) and the Lincoln School (1903) are among the listed buildings
  • The Police and Fire Station dates to 1914 and the library to 1931
  • ZIP code: 01890

Who is responsible for what in a shared building

  • Fixtures inside your unit, such as a sink, toilet or water heater you control, are usually yours to report.
  • Shared drain lines, risers and the main shutoff usually belong to the building owner.
  • Your lease decides the rest, so read it before you pay for a repair you may not owe.

These are general rules of thumb, not legal advice, and a lease can say something different. A plumber can tell you which line is causing the problem, and that finding settles who is expected to call.
