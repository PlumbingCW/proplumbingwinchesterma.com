Winchester Highlands at a glance

Winchester Highlands lies in the northern part of Winchester. Its name comes from a former commuter rail stop on Cross Street, on what is now the MBTA Lowell Line. The stop opened before 1864 as a flag stop called North Winchester and was renamed Winchester Highlands on December 31, 1877. It closed on May 30, 1978, and its shelter no longer stands.

  • When the station opened, North Winchester was a village of about 14 houses
  • Winchester's northern neighbor is Woburn, which has its own page
  • ZIP code: 01890

What we know and what we do not

The Highlands has no formal boundary that we could verify, and we found no reliable survey of its housing. So this page makes no claim about the age of homes here, what is inside their walls or which plumbing problems are common. If you live in the Highlands, you know your house better than any page does, and a plumber should start from what is actually in front of them.
