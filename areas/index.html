How coverage works

Winchester Pro Plumbing is based at 955 Main St, Winchester, MA 01890. The 24/7 line serves Winchester and the towns that border it. We list distances as estimates, and we do not promise arrival times. Instead, the dispatcher confirms your address and tells you what to expect when you call.

The towns below are listed in order of how close they sit to Winchester. Woburn lies directly north, Medford to the south, Arlington to the southwest and Lexington to the west. Each page explains what is different about that town: the water supply, the age of the housing, the ZIP codes and the problems homeowners there call about most.

Winchester itself

Winchester has one standard ZIP code, 01890, and it covers the town from Winchester Center and the Main Street corridor to the Wedgemere district and the West Side. The services pages carry the details for Winchester homes, including the Aberjona River flood history, the older housing eras and the water supply.

Small places around these towns do not get pages of their own, but they are served through the same line. Wilmington borders Woburn to the north. Somerville borders Medford to the south. Call and give the street address, and the dispatcher confirms before sending anyone.

Which page should I read?

Read the page for the town where the property sits, not where you live. A landlord in Arlington with a rental in Medford should use the Medford page. For emergencies, skip the reading and call. The line is open 24/7 and the dispatcher handles the routing.
