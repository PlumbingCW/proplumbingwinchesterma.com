Main sewer line blocked: an emergency that affects the whole house

A blocked branch affects one fixture. A blocked main affects them all. If the basement floor drain gurgles when upstairs toilets flush, or a tub on the lowest level fills when you drain the washer, the stoppage is in the main line.

At that point each flush or load of laundry adds to the overflow. Stop all water use and, if you can reach it safely, find the cleanout fitting. It is typically a capped pipe near the foundation or in the basement. Do not open it yourself if water is high behind it, since the pressure can release sewage onto you.

How a main line is cleaned

The plumber opens the cleanout and sends a heavy-duty cable machine with a cutting head through the line. The head cuts roots, breaks through grease and pushes debris toward the street. For heavy buildup or repeat clogs, the plumber follows with a hydro jet to wash the pipe wall.

Afterward a camera pass checks the result and looks for the cause. Without that step, you are guessing whether the line is clear or merely open. See the sewer camera inspection page for what to ask for.

Why main lines block in Winchester

Winchester has older houses, mature plantings and long runs from some homes to the street. Roots, old clay joints and iron scale are the usual causes. In the town center and apartment buildings, grease and wipes joining from many units can block shared lines. Wipes are a growing problem even when the package says flushable.

Heavy rain is another factor to ask about. In some neighborhoods groundwater and storm flow can enter older lines through cracks, which can overload them. A line that backs up only during storms points at that cause, and a camera check can confirm it. The Aberjona River runs through the town center and the area has a documented history of flooding, so ask about it when a backup follows heavy rain.

Cleanouts: the small fitting that saves money

A cleanout is a capped access pipe that lets the plumber reach the main without removing a toilet. If your house has none, ask about installing one. It makes every future cleaning and camera inspection faster, and it gives you a safe spot to relieve pressure in an emergency.

Cleanout installation is a small plumbing job and often can be done the same visit as the cleaning. It is one of those inexpensive changes that pays for itself the next time the line backs up.

Landlords and multi-family buildings

In two and three family houses a main line backup affects the lowest unit first. Tell the dispatcher how many units share the line, which one is affected and who can give access. Shared stacks mean tenants may need notice before the plumber enters. Keep a record of the call times for the building file. Multi-family buildings in Medford, MA follow the same process.

Plumbing work in Massachusetts falls under the state plumbing code, 248 CMR 10.00, and plumbers are licensed through the Massachusetts Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters. Ask any plumber to confirm their license and which permit the job needs before work starts.
