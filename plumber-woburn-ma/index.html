What Woburn homeowners call about first

Drain and sewer trouble leads the list here. Woburn has a long industrial past, from its first tanneries to the commercial buildings along the Route 128 and Route 93 corridors today. Where a building holds more than one unit or business, a backup can trace to a shared lateral, and the first question is whether the problem is in your branch or in the line you share.

Start with the symptom. If one sink is slow, it is a branch problem for drain cleaning. If the basement floor drain bubbles while upstairs fixtures work, treat it as a main line issue and read about sewer line cleaning. A backup that returns after cabling is the case for hydro jetting.

Water heater failures come next, especially in winter. A tank that leaks gets the same dispatch as a backup. Shut off the cold supply to the heater, turn off power or gas and call. Replacement the same day is offered when appointments and stock are available.

Woburn water: city wells at Horn Pond and the MWRA

Woburn is not served the same way as Winchester. The city draws water from its own wells in the aquifer near Horn Pond, which are treated at the Horn Pond Treatment Plant, and it also receives MWRA water. The city's MWRA community report page names chloramine as the disinfectant. Winchester, by contrast, gets MWRA water plus town reservoirs in the Middlesex Fells.

What this means for you: if your tap water changes, or you want a water quality report, call the Woburn Department of Public Works, not the Winchester Water Department. Plumbers see differences in mineral content between systems, but we do not quote a hardness figure for Woburn here. Test your tap with a hardness strip, and ask the DPW for its current water quality report before sizing a softener or flush schedule.

Before we arrive: burst pipe or active leak in Woburn

These steps keep damage small while the plumber is on the way. Do what you can safely and then call.

Shut off the main

Close the main water valve, usually near the meter in the basement or utility room. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, the panel or appliances, switch off those breakers only if the floor and your hands are dry.

Clear the area

Move rugs, electronics and stored boxes from the wet floor. Keep children and pets out of the room.

Old tanneries and new pipes: what age means in Woburn

Woburn dates to 1640, and its early tanneries date to the 1600s. That history does not mean your pipes are old. It does mean the city has housing from many eras, and in older blocks you may find original cast iron stacks, clay laterals and galvanized supply lines next to newer copper and PVC. A camera inspection shows which you have.
