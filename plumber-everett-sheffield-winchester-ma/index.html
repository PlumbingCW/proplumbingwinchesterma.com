The district at a glance

The Everett Avenue and Sheffield Road Historic District was listed on the National Register on July 5, 1989. It is roughly triangular: Bacon Street forms the north edge, Church Street, Sheffield West and Sheffield Road form the west edge, and Upper Mystic Lake and Mystic Valley Parkway form the south and east edges.

  • 129 properties, 20 of which do not contribute to the district's historic significance
  • Most houses built between 1890 and 1916, with the earliest, at 8 Everett Avenue, dating to 1880
  • Colonial Revival and Bungalow/Craftsman styles are listed for the district

Living beside the Mystic Lakes

The Aberjona River and its ponds flow toward the Mystic Lakes on Winchester's southern border, and this district sits on the lake edge. Medford and Arlington are the neighboring towns on the far side of the lakes.

We have no flood or groundwater data for these specific streets, so we will not tell you that your basement is at risk. What follows is general information for any house near water, and it is worth reading once even if your basement has always been dry.

Sump pumps and basement water: the general picture

A sump pump moves water out of a pit in the basement floor so it does not collect. Whether you need one depends on your own house and lot, and a plumber can look at the basement and tell you. If you already have one, three checks take ten minutes:

  • Lift the lid and pour a bucket of water into the pit. The pump should start, empty it and shut off.
  • Follow the discharge pipe outside and make sure it runs clear and ends well away from the foundation.
  • Ask whether the pump has battery backup, because heavy storms are when power outages tend to happen.

If the pump fails the test, a sump pump visit is the next step. If water shows up even with a working pump, the conversation widens to basement waterproofing, which is a bigger job and should come with a written scope.

Drains and sewer lines in houses from 1890 to 1916

The age of the houses here does not prove anything about the lines under them, and some will have been replaced. What age does justify is a look before you assume. A slow main drain or a floor drain that gurgles can be cleared with drain cleaning, and a sewer camera inspection shows what is actually in the line instead of leaving you to guess.
