When a burst pipe is an emergency

Any pipe that is spraying, pouring or dripping steadily is an emergency, because water spreads into walls and floors faster than most people expect. Work through these steps while you reach the phone:

  1. Turn off the main water valve, usually near where the line enters the basement
  2. Switch off the breaker for wet rooms if you can reach the panel safely and your hands and floor are dry
  3. Open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the pipes and relieve pressure
  4. Photograph the damage for your insurance claim before cleaning up
  5. Call the 24/7 line and report the location of the leak

If you cannot find the main valve, or it will not turn, say that on the call. The plumber can also close the street side shutoff in many cases, or advise you on what to do next.

Why pipes burst in Winchester

Cold is the headline cause. Water expands as it freezes, and the pressure builds between the ice and a closed faucet. The pipe often splits where the ice formed, but the leak appears only after the thaw. Exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces, garages and the corners of older houses are the weak spots.

Age is the second cause. Houses of the age found in the Wedgemere area and the older streets near the center can still have galvanized steel supply lines that rust from the inside, and old copper that pinholes. If your home has had a repair before, another failure near it is likely. See our whole house repiping service if the plumber finds more than one weak section.

Frozen pipes before they burst

A faucet that runs dry on a cold morning means ice is in the line. Open the tap, leave it open and warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer, working from the faucet toward the frozen section. Never use a torch or open flame. If you can see a bulge or a crack, shut off the water and call before thawing, since thawing a split pipe starts the flood.

Also check the spot where the pipe enters the house. An exterior wall with no insulation behind the sink base cabinet is a classic location. Open the cabinet door overnight in a cold snap to let house air reach the pipes.

Steps after the water is off

Once the pipe is repaired, slow down the cleanup. Wet drywall and insulation can grow mold within a couple of days, so ask the plumber about drying and call a restoration company if more than a small area is wet. Keep receipts, photos and the plumber's written invoice for your insurer.

Burst pipe coverage in most homeowner policies is for sudden and accidental damage, and denials are common when the claim looks like slow leakage over months. Prevention before the next cold snap

Insulate exposed pipes in garages and crawlspaces, seal drafts around pipe penetrations and set the thermostat no lower than 55 degrees if you travel. Know where your main shutoff is and test that it turns. A valve that has not moved in ten years often will not close in an emergency, and replacing it is a small job that can save a large one.

Plumbing work in Massachusetts falls under the state plumbing code, 248 CMR 10.00, and plumbers are licensed through the Massachusetts Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters. Ask any plumber to confirm their license and which permit the job needs before work starts.
