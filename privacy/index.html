Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you call us or visit this website. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Talk to a plumber

Call Now

Information from phone calls

When you call (339) 216-9771, we may collect your phone number, your name, your service address and a description of the problem. We use this to dispatch a plumber, to confirm coverage, to follow up on the service and to improve our service.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want to be recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Information from this website

This site has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance.

The site embeds Google Maps. When a map loads, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not control that data.

How we use and share information

  • To dispatch and schedule plumbing service
  • To contact you about a request you made
  • To improve our service and train our staff
  • To comply with the law

We share information with the licensed plumbing contractors who perform the work, and with service providers who help us operate the line. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete or correct information we hold about you by emailing contact@proplumbingwinchesterma.com or calling (339) 216-9771. We respond as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom of the page shows when it was last changed. Questions about this policy can be sent to the email address above or to 955 Main St, Winchester, MA 01890.
